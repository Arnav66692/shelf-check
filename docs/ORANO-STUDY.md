Measured in Chrome at 1736x966 on https://www.orano.group/experience/innovation/en/slider. Plain fetch is blocked by a Barracuda firewall, so use a real browser.

## Tokens

- Background #05070F, flat. Faint star specks drift.
- Grid: thin hairlines, white at about 6 percent. Vertical lines at about 18, 38, 60 and 82 percent of the width, horizontal at about 31 and 69 percent of the height.
- Accent yellow #FFE600. Body text #CFCFCF. Inactive labels #898989.
- Title font Blender Pro weight 100, 60px, letter-spacing 12px, uppercase, line-height 72px. Each letter has red #FF0000, green #0AFF15 and yellow #FFE600 copies that flicker for an RGB split on change.
- Body Nunito Sans 300, 16px, line-height 28.8px, max width about 486px.
- Labels Blender Pro 500, 16px, letter-spacing 2.4px (0.15em), uppercase.
- ENTER button: white block, dark text #161611, Blender Pro 500 uppercase, a yellow bar down its left edge, about 194x50.
- Slide counter at top centre: "01 —— 02", current number yellow and blinking.
- Round prev and next buttons, 70px canvas rings, at the left and right edges at mid height.
- Top left: yellow bar plus small uppercase line. Top right: small uppercase menu link. Bottom left: logo. Bottom right: language, sound wave, return link.

## Scene

- Each slide is a white wireframe 3D model of the real object (gamma camera, AR tablet, crane cab) on a perspective grid floor, sitting left of centre.
- Yellow HUD callout boxes outline parts of the model.
- The model fades in as lines, the title letters scramble in with the RGB split.

## Shelf Check mapping

- Slides become the app's tools: Cabinet (pill bottle), Symptoms (heart line), Pill finder (capsule), Dose check (syringe), Peptides (DNA helix).
- Our own models are drawn in original code. Nothing from Orano is copied: fonts are free stand-ins (Saira for Blender Pro, Nunito Sans is already free), no models, copy, audio or logo.
